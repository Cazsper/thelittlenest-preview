The Story · 002

Bottles in.
Bedding out.

It starts with a bottle, and ends somewhere much nicer. Recycled PET plastic bottles are transformed into soft, lofty DreamGreen fibre, giving them a new life as the filling inside our duvets, toppers, pillows and protectors.

Comfort doesn't have to come at the expense of practicality. DreamGreen is wonderfully soft and breathable, springs back beautifully after washing and is made to keep its loft and comfort night after night. Encased in a 100% cotton outer, it feels natural, gentle and inviting against the skin.

And for those who prefer a little peace of mind, DreamGreen is hypoallergenic and free from the common allergens associated with natural fills such as down and wool. It's a simple, thoughtful choice for a comfortable home, and a little kinder to the world we live in.

What It Means · 003

Good linen
shouldn't cost the earth.

We believe comfort and conscious choices can live beautifully together. By choosing recycled fibres, we can create bedding that feels every bit as soft, comfortable and lasting as you'd expect, while giving materials that might otherwise become waste a new purpose.

It's a small change, but one that matters. Fewer new resources, less waste and more thoughtful choices · all wrapped up in something as simple and lovely as a good night's sleep.
