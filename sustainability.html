The Story · 002

Bottles in.
Bedding out.

Every DreamGreen product starts life as recycled PET plastic bottles. Through a fibre conversion process, those bottles become the fill inside your duvets, toppers, pillows and protectors — soft, lofty, and built to survive the washing cycle that hospitality demands.

It performs the same as virgin fibre. It washes warm. It lofts back to shape. The fill is encased in a 100% cotton outer — breathable, soft, and gentle against skin. The only difference is where it came from.

DreamGreen fill is hypo-allergenic — a common question from guests with allergies or sensitive skin. It's free from the allergens associated with natural fills like down and wool.

What It Means · 003

Good linen
shouldn't cost the earth.

Hospitality bedding takes a beating. It gets washed constantly, replaced regularly, and disposed of at scale. Using recycled fibre doesn't change how the product performs — it just means fewer virgin resources go into something that will spend its life in a laundry cycle.
