Returns & Exchanges · 02

Returns & exchanges.

We want you to be satisfied with your order. If something isn't right, contact us and we'll work it out.

Change of mind: We accept returns on unused, unopened items in original packaging within 7 days of delivery. Return freight is at your cost. Once we receive and inspect the item, we'll issue a refund to your original payment method within 5 working days. We can't accept any product back once the packaging has been opened, for hygiene reasons.

Transit damage: If your order arrives damaged as a result of transport, this must be reported within 24 hours of delivery. Email us a photo and your order number and we'll arrange a replacement or refund.

Faulty goods, production issues, or incorrect items: The 7-day and 24-hour windows do not apply to faulty goods, items with production defects, or orders where we have sent the wrong product. These must be reported within 14 days of delivery. We'll cover return freight and replace or refund in full — your choice. This applies regardless of whether the item has been opened.

Mattresses and bases: Mattresses and bases cannot be returned for change of mind. In exceptional circumstances, returns may be considered on a case-by-case basis with prior approval from us. If approved, the customer is responsible for all freight costs both ways.

To start a return or report an issue, email sales@thelittlenest.co.nz with your order number and reason. We'll reply within one working day.

Privacy Policy · 03

Privacy policy.

The Little Nest ("we", "us") is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

What we collect: When you place an order or contact us, we collect your name, delivery address, email address, and phone number. We may also collect information about your order history and preferences.

How we use it: We use your information to process and deliver your orders, communicate with you about your order, and provide customer service. We do not use your information for unsolicited marketing without your consent.

Who we share it with: We share your delivery details with NZ Couriers for the purpose of delivering your order. We use Stripe to process payments — they handle your card details securely and we never store them. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Storage & security: Your information is stored securely. We take reasonable steps to protect it from unauthorised access, loss, or disclosure.

Your rights: Under the Privacy Act 2020, you have the right to access and correct personal information we hold about you. To make a request, email sales@thelittlenest.co.nz

Cookies: Our website may use cookies to improve your browsing experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

For any privacy enquiries, contact us at sales@thelittlenest.co.nz. Last updated May 2026.
